AQA CHEM · PAPER 2June 2024

4 Rates, equilibria and pH

A student investigated the reaction between ethanoic acid and ethanol. The mixture was left to reach equilibrium at 298 K in a sealed flask.

4(a) State what is meant by the term dynamic equilibrium and explain why the concentrations of the reactants and products remain constant.

4(b) Explain the effect of adding a catalyst on the value of Kc for this reaction. Justify your answer with reference to the rates of the forward and reverse reactions.

4(c) The equilibrium mixture was cooled to 273 K. Deduce whether the forward reaction is exothermic, using the observation that the yield of ester increased.

5 The pH of a 0.100 mol dm⁻³ solution of a weak acid HA was measured as 2.88 at 298 K.

UNIT 4 · NOTES

Rates of reaction

6 blocks · 12 cards · linked to Equilibria map
•Collision theory: particles must collide with energy ≥ Ea and in the correct orientation.
•Rate ∝ frequency of successful collisions.
–Temperature shifts the Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution to the right; the area past Ea grows faster than the mean.
–A catalyst lowers Ea by providing an alternative route.
FLASHCARD · IN THIS DOC
What are the two conditions for a successful collision?
Sufficient energy (≥ Ea) and correct orientation.

ELECTROPHILIC ADDITION
Why does Markovnikov's rule favour the more substituted carbocation?
Alkyl groups are electron-donating, so they stabilise the positive charge through induction and hyperconjugation. The lower-energy intermediate forms faster, so its product dominates.
