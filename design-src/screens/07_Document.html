Rates of reaction

6 blocks · 12 cards · linked to Equilibria map
•Collision theory: particles must collide with energy ≥ Ea and in the correct orientation.
•Rate ∝ frequency of successful collisions.
–Temperature shifts the Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution to the right; the area past Ea grows faster than the mean.
–A catalyst lowers Ea by providing an alternative route.

What are the two conditions for a successful collision?
Sufficient energy (≥ Ea) and correct orientation.
